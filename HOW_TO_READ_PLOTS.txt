HOW TO READ THE AHU–VAV DIAGNOSTIC PLOTS
August 2026 data | Prepared 28 September 2026

FILES AND ORDER
There are three pages for each VAV, in VAV01–VAV10 order:
  01_month: full-month time series and sustained-event markers.
  02_relationships: three scatter plots using the full month.
  03_detail: a selected time window at the original 15-minute resolution.
The PDF contains these 30 pages. The HTML gallery and notebook Section 5
contain the same plots. Individual PNG files can be opened at full size.
Titles, axis labels and legends identify the data. Explanations are here,
outside the charts.

START WITH THE FULL-MONTH PAGE
Read vertically at the same timestamp across the panels:
  1. Blue: zone/room temperature. Black dashed: recorded controller target.
     Green band: +/-1 degrees C around that target.
  2. Teal: measured airflow in CFM. Black dashed: configured minimum.
     Orange dotted: 80% of configured minimum.
     Purple dotted: max(50 CFM,
     10% of configured maximum), used by the near-closed rule.
     A gray dash-dot maximum-flow line appears only if within the plotted
     airflow range. All configured maxima are listed at the end of this file.
     Airflow axes adapt to each VAV/window; check scales before comparing.
  3. Purple: recorded damper indication (%).
     Dotted lines: near-closed 5% and open 95% thresholds.
  4. Black steps: recorded AHU-3 fan status (On or Off).
     Green dots at the 'Eligible' level identify screened samples.
     'Eligible' is a data-analysis marker, not a third fan state.
  5. Red squares: samples belonging to a sustained screening condition.
     Each row is a separate rule. Rules can overlap at the same time.
     Gray shading in this bottom panel means excluded data, not normal data.

TIMELINE SHADING
Gray shading in the temperature/airflow/damper panels means recorded fan Off.
Pink shading means suspected frozen data: all three measurements are exactly
unchanged across a continuous run spanning at least 120 minutes.
The two shades may overlap. Raw lines are still displayed in these periods.
Shading extends each sampled interval by 15 minutes for display; exact change
times between samples are unknown. Lines between samples are visual guides.

READ THE RELATIONSHIP PAGE
Each dot is one timestamp; the scatter plots alone do not establish duration.
Light-gray dots are excluded from screening (Off/startup/invalid/frozen).
Colored dots are eligible samples.

Left: damper (%) on the x-axis, measured airflow (CFM) on the y-axis.
Color is zone temperature minus controller setpoint: red is warmer, blue is
colder, and near-neutral colors are close to target. Colors saturate at +/-4 C;
this does not clip the original measurements.
Vertical dotted lines mark 5% and 95% damper. The purple horizontal
line is the significant-flow threshold. Black dashed is configured minimum.

Center: temperature error on the x-axis, airflow on the y-axis.
Right: temperature error on the x-axis, damper indication on the y-axis.
Center/right colors indicate damper %, using the rightmost colorbar.
In both panels, positive error is warmer than target; negative is colder.
The green vertical band is +/-1 C, and black dashed vertical is zero error.
A visible cluster is a reason to inspect its timeline, not proof of a fault.

READ THE DETAIL PAGE
The same four raw-signal panels are shown at a shorter timescale.
Orange vertical dashed lines bound the first and last sample in the selected
sustained event. Two hours of surrounding context are included where available.
The title gives the displayed dates; the x-axis gives times.
The selected rule is listed under DETAIL WINDOWS below, rather than written
over the chart. Only one selected window per VAV is shown, so consult the
full-month panel and diagnostic_events.csv for other events.

VAV09 has no eligible samples because its three signals are constant across
the dataset. Its relationship page contains gray points only. Its detail page
shows a 48-hour window around the first recorded fan-On, with no event markers.
This absence of colored points is not evidence that the VAV is healthy.

SCREENING RULES (ADJUSTABLE ANALYST THRESHOLDS)
Near-closed + flow: damper <= 5% and flow greater than the purple threshold.
Open + low flow: damper >= 95% and flow < 80% of configured minimum.
Below configured minimum: flow < 80% of configured minimum.
Above configured maximum: flow > 120% of configured maximum.
Warm zone: zone temperature minus recorded target > 1 C.
Cold zone: zone temperature minus recorded target < -1 C.
Warm + low damper: warm-zone rule and damper <= 20%.
Warm + open damper: warm-zone rule and damper >= 95%.
Cold + above minimum: cold-zone rule and flow > 120% of minimum.

Eligibility requires recorded fan On, at least 30 minutes since the recorded
run started, valid required values/ranges, and no suspected frozen triplet.
Each condition must persist at consecutive 15-minute timestamps spanning at
least 45 minutes (four samples with current settings). Gaps and exclusions
break events. A run starting at the dataset boundary also has a conservative
startup exclusion because its earlier state is unknown.

HOW TO INTERPRET THE EVIDENCE
1. Locate a red event marker in the full-month page.
2. Read the damper, airflow and temperature panels at that same time.
3. Verify the fan status and absence of frozen-data shading.
4. Inspect the detailed window or event CSV for duration and context.
5. Compare other VAVs at the same time to distinguish a shared pattern.

Examples of investigation leads, not confirmed causes:
- Near-closed indication with sustained flow: check actual damper closure,
  actuator/point mapping, leakage and airflow calibration.
- High opening with low flow: check actual air delivery, duct pressure,
  restrictions and airflow sensing.
- Warm zone with low opening: check local demand/control response and target.
- Cold zone with above-minimum flow: review cooling control and target.
If many VAVs change together, check shared AHU operation and timing first.

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION LIMITS
Fan state is taken directly from CHN_CKC_B1_03F_BW_FAH03_$FAN_RST.
It is not inferred from airflow and is not yet verified as physical feedback.
The damper signal may also be a command rather than actual blade position.
STMP_CEL is treated as zone temperature, as clarified by the user.
Recorded STMP_SET values are the comparison targets. Layout temperatures are
provisional design values and are not substituted into this analysis.
Configured airflow limits are not instantaneous airflow demand. Occupancy,
active zone mode, supply temperature and duct pressure are unavailable here.
Zero airflow is not automatically missing data. Frozen signals require review.
Percentages in the diagnostic tables use eligible samples as denominators;
different VAVs have different valid coverage. Do not add overlapping rule rates.
No efficiency score, compliance determination or confirmed fault is implied.
Timestamps are used as recorded; the workbook does not encode a timezone.
